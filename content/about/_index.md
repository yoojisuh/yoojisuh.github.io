**Welcome!**

I am Yoo Ji Suh (Yooji), a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Journalism and Mass Communication at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. I was a University Fellow at UW–Madison and part of the [Social Media and Democracy (SMAD)](https://mcrc.journalism.wisc.edu/groups/smad/) and [Center for Communication and Civic Renewal (CCCR)](https://cccr.wisc.edu/) research groups. 

My research brings together moral psychology and political communication. I study how moral values and emotions shape the way people engage with politics in digital media environments — and how those environments, in turn, shape what people believe, feel, and value. I have published ten peer-reviewed articles, four as lead author, in venues such as *Communication Research*, *Digital Journalism*, *Media and Communication*, and *Communication Quarterly*.

To tackle these questions, I use a mix of methods, including computational content analysis, surveys and experiments, linkages between media content and survey data, and large language model (LLM) simulations.
